Citizen Application & Financial Literacy Handbook

1. NPCI Aadhaar Seeding for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Linking a bank account with Aadhaar is distinct from NPCI Mapper seeding. All central and state DBT disbursements (PM-KISAN, LPG subsidy, post-matric scholarships) route funds via the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge. Citizens should verify active NPCI bank mapping before submitting online scheme applications.

2. Margin Money Subsidy vs. Credit-Linked Interest Subvention

Industrial and MSME schemes provide capital assistance in two primary forms: upfront Margin Money Grants (such as PMEGP offering 15% to 35% of project cost adjusted after a 3-year lock-in) and Interest Subvention (where the ministry reimburses 3% to 6% p.a. on regular term-loan repayments) paired with CGTMSE collateral-free credit guarantees.

3. Public Procurement MSE Benefits on GeM & CPPP

Under the Public Procurement Policy for Micro and Small Enterprises Order, Central Ministries and PSUs mandate at least 25% annual procurement from MSEs (including 4% from SC/ST-owned and 3% from women-owned MSEs), alongside exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) for Udyam-registered enterprises.

4. Protecting Against Fraudulent Middlemen & Fake Portals

Authentic Government of India portals use official .gov.in or .nic.in domains and never demand commission fees via personal UPI links or WhatsApp messages to sanction welfare benefits. Always cross-check the official source URL listed on each SarkarSaathi profile.
